About HiYa

Helping Students Prepare for Their Global Future

HiYa Education Support and Finance is focused on providing student visa related financial documentation support to students planning to study abroad.

  • Student Visa Financial Documentation Support
  • Bank Statement & FDR Assistance
  • Fund Documentation Guidance
  • Study Abroad Support

Our Approach

We aim to make the documentation preparation process simple, clear and organized so students can focus on their education plans.

All applications and documents should meet the relevant requirements of the destination country and institution.
